Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about ordering or registering your store? We've got answers.

Is LocalBiz free for local shop owners to register?
Yes! LocalBiz is 100% free with zero commission fees for local merchants. Store owners can launch their digital storefront, add products, and receive direct customer orders via WhatsApp or dashboard in minutes.
How does prescription medicine delivery work for pharmacies?
When visiting licensed medical stores like Kusum Medical Store, customers can click "Upload Prescription" to send a photo of their doctor's note. The licensed local pharmacist reviews the note before dispatching medicines safely.
How fast is delivery from local neighborhood stores?
Because orders go directly to nearby stores within your 2km radius, most shop owners deliver your essentials, fresh groceries, or medicines within 20 to 30 minutes!
